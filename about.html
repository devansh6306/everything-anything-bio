Your guide

Mito the Mighty, the Happy Mitochondria

Inside almost every cell in your body are hundreds of tiny power stations called mitochondria. They take the food you eat and the air you breathe and turn them into ATP, the energy your cells spend on everything you do.

Mito is one of them, and a very cheerful one. Mito wears goggles for lab safety, glows with energy, and is always happy to stop and explain. Look for Mito's speech boxes in every post.
